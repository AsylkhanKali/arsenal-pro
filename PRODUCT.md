# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Procurement officers and decision-makers in Kazakhstan government agencies
(Ministry of Defense, National Guard, police, border service). They arrive to
verify the company is real and credible before contact; language may be Russian,
Kazakh, English, Turkish or Chinese.

## Product Purpose

Single-page multilingual brochure ("визитка") for ТОО «Арсенал про Групп», a
defense **supplier** (not manufacturer). No catalog, no prices — the page exists
to build institutional credibility and produce a phone call or email.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, field-grade, restrained. Military-professional without cosplay:
camo texture and tactical cues stay subtle; the tone is a contractor's dossier,
not a video game.

## Anti-references

- Any copy claiming manufacturing, repair, modernization or a "full cycle" —
  hard constraint from the owner, scrubbed from all 5 locales.
- Full-bleed "statement bands" between sections (tried twice, removed twice).
- Uniform card-grid walls repeated per section; sections keep distinct shapes.
- Flashy gamer/FPS aesthetics, aggressive imagery of armored vehicles or K9.

## Design Principles

1. Credibility over spectacle: every element should read as verifiable fact.
2. Supplier framing always: sourcing, logistics, turnkey delivery.
3. Section rhythm: each section has its own layout texture, never a repeated grid.
4. Content is never gated on JS: reveals enhance, no-JS visitors see everything.
5. Real business data (BIN, director, contacts) stays real — no placeholders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animation. Contrast per WCAG AA on the
dark olive palette. 5 locales with correct hreflang; zh falls back to system CJK
fonts.
